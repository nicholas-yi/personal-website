Outside the brackets

I write sidequests into my main life journey:

  • a recipe untried,
  • a trail less taken,
  • a topic unlearned,
  • a film unreviewed.

so whether it be

  • therapeutic archery as an ex-olympic training team usa member,
  • or my archival art volunteering at stanford's cantor arts center,
  • or developing film as an aspiring hobbyist photographer,
  • or engaging with online open-source data communities.

I seek to understand the outside world one different lens, or neural layer, at a time

If any of those are your thing, say hello ↗.
