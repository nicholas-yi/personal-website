The local files are 42-second garden and 50-second forest excerpts, lightly high-/low-pass filtered, level-balanced, and encoded at 128 kbps stereo MP3. They total about 1.5 MB. They are self-hosted; visitors never download audio from the source mirror. Runtime decoding applies a two-second equal-power overlap to each loop.

Both recordings now run exactly 25% slower than the previous tuning: garden around 0.885x, leaves around 0.96x, with slow, bounded variation (0.8625-0.9075x and 0.93-0.99x). A low-pass filter and master gain retain the hover-shelter muffling. Natural droplets replace synthesized ambient drips; interaction sounds remain generated locally.

Sound defaults off on every page, with a crossed-out speaker icon and an "Enable sound" label. Only activating that sound control (click, tap, Enter, or Space) opts in; unrelated clicks, typing, and graph interaction never start audio. No audio context is created or recordings requested before opt-in. The choice is not persisted across navigation or reload. Muting remains available while recordings load. Failed requests/decode operations show an explicit error rather than silently reverting to synthetic noise. The sound context suspends while muted or the tab is hidden.

The separate visual rain preference is remembered in tab-scoped `sessionStorage` for the visit. With no saved visit preferences and no OS reduced-motion request, the defaults are lamp off, rain motion on, and sound off. Pausing rain also keeps decorative weather motion paused on other pages, but does not disable the manually activated glass wipe. Reduced motion remains the automatic rain default even if a visit had previously enabled rain; an explicit paused choice also survives later OS-preference changes. BFCache restoration re-reads the visit choice. No sound setting is saved. Storage denial or malformed preferences are logged and surfaced in the existing status area; the current page's control remains usable.

The separate lamp/daylight control selects a manual reading palette. A fresh tab-scoped visit starts in daylight with the lamp off, and sound always starts off. Lamplight uses warm charcoal, parchment, amber, and olive with a static pool of warmth around the room and hearth. An explicit palette choice is stored only in `sessionStorage`, so it persists across pages and reloads during that visit, independently of the rain setting. Old cross-visit `localStorage` palette values are ignored; no clock, location, or automatic dark-mode selection can enable the lamp. A small head script restores the current visit's palette before CSS paints, including BFCache restoration. Denied/invalid storage is logged and announced without disabling the toggle.

Lamplight covers reading text, houses, graph ink and states, popovers, copy controls, cursor artwork, and weather controls without inverting photographs, album covers, or the official Spotify player.
